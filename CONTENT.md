# 55 Malin Road — Content Governance

## Core Principle

> When in doubt, remove rather than add. Every idea should appear once, every section should have a single purpose, and the page should feel lighter after every revision.

---

## Section Ownership

Each section owns its content. No section should duplicate another.

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Hero | Introduce the property and enable immediate booking |
| Introduction | Explain the house |
| Living | Show shared spaces |
| Bedrooms | Show accommodation |
| Kitchen | Cooking and dining |
| Outdoor | Garden, hot tub and BBQ |
| Facilities | Practical information only |
| Your base in Moville | Transition from house to destination |
| Life Here | Everyday Moville |
| Explore | Wider Inishowen |
| Booking | Reservations and enquiries |
| Footer | Brand, navigation and final conversion |

If content belongs elsewhere, move it rather than repeat it.

---

## Eliminate Repetition

Every piece of information should appear **once, in the section where it has the greatest impact**.

**The house sleeps six** — mention once (Introduction).

**Walking distance to town** — mention once (Introduction).

**Hot tub** — describe in Outdoor. List once in Facilities. Avoid elsewhere.

**Three bedrooms** — describe once. Facilities may summarise. Do not repeat in multiple paragraphs.

**Explore Inishowen** — keep destination content within Explore. Do not repeat attractions in Life Here.

**Moville** — Life Here sells the town. The hero simply positions it geographically.

**Booking** — Hero → Booking section → Footer. Avoid repetitive booking prompts throughout.

---

## One Message Per Section

- Hero → This is 55 Malin Road.
- Introduction → Here's what the house offers.
- Living → Shared space.
- Bedrooms → Sleeping arrangements.
- Outdoor → Relax outdoors.
- Facilities → Practical details.
- Your base in Moville → Why the location matters.
- Life Here → Experience the town.
- Explore → Discover the peninsula.
- Footer → Ready to stay?

---

## Copy Quality

Every sentence should earn its place. If removing a sentence does not reduce understanding or confidence, remove it.

- Fewer words.
- More whitespace.
- Stronger photography.
- Clearer hierarchy.
