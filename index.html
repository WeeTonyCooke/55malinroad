55 Malin Road — Inishowen, Co. Donegal

A house at the edge
of everything.

There is a particular quality to the light here. It arrives sideways in the morning, catches the limestone, and disappears into the Atlantic by afternoon. 55 Malin Road sits at the tip of the Inishowen Peninsula — a place where the road eventually runs out, and something else begins.

We offer two rooms. No television. Good coffee. An open fire when the season calls for it.

The House

Two rooms. Each one different.
Both facing the Atlantic.

Room 01

The Atlantic Room

A large room with a west-facing window. On clear evenings you can watch the sun drop into the sea from bed. On stormy mornings, the window is even better. Stone walls, linen, a reading chair that has earned its place.

Bed King, Portuguese linen
Outlook Atlantic, facing west
From €140 per night
Room 02

The Garden Room

Quieter. Faces the garden and the hill behind the house. The light here is softer — better for long mornings, better for reading, better for the kind of sleep you come all this way to find.

Bed Double, Portuguese linen
Outlook Garden & hill, facing east
From €120 per night
