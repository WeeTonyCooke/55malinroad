here.

Moville is a small town with a beautiful waterfront, a handful of good places to eat and drink, and a community that runs a fun-packed festival each summer.

Shore Walk

Days worth
taking.

Five Finger Strand is about half an hour. Culdaff Surf School is about fifteen. Malin Head, about forty. Cross the Foyle by ferry and head for the Giant's Causeway, the Antrim Coast or the famous Carrick-a-Rede Rope Bridge. On clear nights, Inishowen is one of Ireland's best places to catch a glimpse of the aurora — breathtaking!
