Settle in.

55 Malin Road is a comfortable three-bedroom holiday home in the heart of Moville. With space for up to six guests, it's an easy place to settle in, whether you're here for a weekend or a longer stay.

The town's waterfront, cafés, pubs and local shops are all within walking distance, while the wider Inishowen Peninsula is right on your doorstep.
