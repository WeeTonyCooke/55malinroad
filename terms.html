Booking terms

Terms & conditions.

These terms apply to stays booked directly with us. Bookings made through Airbnb or Booking.com are governed by that platform's own terms and cancellation policy, which may differ from what follows.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Making a booking

A booking is confirmed only once we have written confirmation to you by email and the deposit has been received. An enquiry through this website, by phone or by message is not a confirmed booking, and dates are not held until the deposit clears.

The person making the booking must be at least 18 and takes responsibility for everyone in the party, including payment and the condition of the house.

2. Payment

  • A deposit of 25% of the total is payable to confirm the booking.
  • The balance is due 14 days before arrival.
  • For bookings made within 14 days of arrival, the full amount is payable at the time of booking.
  • Payment is by bank transfer unless we agree otherwise.

If the balance is not received by the due date we may treat the booking as cancelled by you, and the cancellation terms below will apply.

3. Minimum stay

Two nights minimum. Longer minimums may apply at Christmas, New Year and bank holiday weekends; we'll tell you at the time of enquiry if so.

4. Cancellation by you

  • More than 28 days before arrival — full refund of everything paid.
  • 14 to 28 days before arrival — deposit retained, any balance paid refunded.
  • Fewer than 14 days before arrival — no refund.

If we are able to re-let the dates, we will refund what we recover, less any shortfall in rate and a €25 administration charge. We strongly recommend travel insurance covering cancellation.

Bookings of accommodation for a specific date are exempt from the 14-day cooling-off period that applies to most distance contracts under Irish and EU consumer law. The terms above are the applicable cancellation terms.

5. Cancellation by us

We would only cancel in exceptional circumstances — serious damage, a failure of essential services, or events outside our control. If we do, you will receive a full refund of everything paid. Our liability is limited to that refund and we are not responsible for other costs such as travel or onward bookings.

6. Arrival and departure

Check-in is from 3pm, by self check-in using a lockbox. Check-out is by 12 noon. Earlier arrival or later departure can sometimes be arranged — please ask rather than assume, as the house often needs the full window for changeover.

7. Occupancy

The house sleeps six and six is the maximum, including children and infants. Additional visitors may not stay overnight. Day visitors are fine in reasonable numbers; parties and events are not permitted, and we may end a stay without refund where this is breached.

8. The hot tub

The hot tub is available year-round and is used entirely at your own risk. By booking you accept the following on behalf of everyone in your party:

  • Children must be supervised by an adult at all times and should not use the tub unaccompanied.
  • No glass of any kind in or near the tub.
  • Do not use the tub while under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Anyone who is pregnant, has heart trouble or high or low blood pressure, or is taking medication, should take medical advice before use.
  • Please shower before entering and do not use bath products in the tub.
  • Keep the cover on when the tub is not in use.
  • Do not adjust the temperature, chemical levels or any part of the equipment.

The tub is drained, cleaned and refilled between stays and may occasionally be unavailable for maintenance or on safety grounds. Where that happens we will tell you as soon as we can. No refund is due for short periods of unavailability, and misuse requiring an unscheduled drain and refill will be charged.

9. Care of the house

Please treat the house as you would your own. You are responsible for any damage or breakage beyond ordinary wear, and we may charge for repair or replacement. Please tell us about breakages rather than leaving us to find them — accidents happen and are usually not charged.

Before you leave

  • Gather used towels.
  • Put rubbish and recycling out.
  • Turn off lights, heating and appliances.
  • Return the keys to the lockbox.
  • Lock up.

10. Smoking and pets

The house is entirely non-smoking, including vaping, and this extends to the bedrooms and the enclosed patio area. Smoking indoors will be charged for at the cost of specialist cleaning. Pets are not permitted. Registered assistance dogs are welcome — please tell us when you book.

11. Noise and neighbours

55 Malin Road is a home in a residential street with neighbours either side. Please keep noise down after 11pm, particularly outside and at the hot tub, where sound carries further than you would think.

12. What's included

Bed linen, towels, heating, electricity, Wi-Fi, use of the hot tub and an initial supply of basics are all included in the rate — there is no additional charge for the hot tub. Beach towels for the hot tub are provided; please don't take the bath towels outside. A travel cot and highchair are available on request.

13. Liability

We are not liable for loss or damage to your belongings, or for personal injury, except where caused by our negligence. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected.

14. Gift vouchers

Vouchers are valid for five years from the date of purchase, as required by the Consumer Protection (Gift Vouchers) Act 2019. There is no requirement to spend the full value in a single booking, more than one voucher may be used together, and we will confirm the expiry date to you in writing. Vouchers are not exchangeable for cash and are applied against the cost of a stay.

15. Your information

When you make an enquiry or booking we collect your name, email address, the dates and party size, and anything you tell us in your message. We use it only to answer your enquiry and manage your stay. We do not sell it or use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

Enquiries submitted through this website are processed by Netlify, our hosting provider. We keep booking records for six years to meet Revenue requirements, and delete unsuccessful enquiries after twelve months. You may ask to see, correct or delete what we hold by writing to hello@55malinroad.ie, and you may complain to the Data Protection Commission. Our full privacy policy sets out the detail.

16. Complaints

If something is wrong during your stay, please tell us straight away so we have the chance to put it right. We can rarely do much about a problem first raised after you've gone home.

17. Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of Ireland and subject to the jurisdiction of the Irish courts.
