Privacy

Your information.

This site collects as little as it can: what you type into the enquiry form, and nothing else. There are no cookies, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. What follows is the detail.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who is responsible

55 Malin Road, Moville, Co. Donegal, Ireland, is the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at hello@55malinroad.ie.

2. What we collect

Only what the enquiry form asks for: your name, your email address, your arrival and departure dates, the size of your party, and anything you choose to write in the message field. If you are requesting a gift voucher, we also record which voucher you asked about.

That is the whole list. We do not ask for a phone number, a postal address or payment details through this website, and we never ask for card details by email.

3. Why we need it

To answer your enquiry and, if you go on to book, to arrange your stay. That is the only use. The lawful basis is that processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and afterwards to perform it.

We do not use your details for marketing, we do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell or share them with anyone for their own purposes.

4. Who else handles it

Netlify hosts this website and receives enquiries submitted through the form. Submissions are stored in our account there. Netlify is a United States company and transfers are covered by its standard data protection terms.

Telegram delivers a notification to us when an enquiry arrives, so that we see it promptly. The notification repeats what you submitted. Telegram is outside the European Economic Area.

Nobody else receives it. If you book through Airbnb instead, that booking is governed by Airbnb's own privacy policy rather than this one.

5. Cookies, analytics and tracking

This site sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, no advertising pixels, no session recording and no fingerprinting. Nothing is stored in your browser, so there is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

The typefaces are served from this domain rather than from a font service, so loading a page does not disclose your visit to a third party. Your web server request is logged by Netlify in the ordinary course of serving the site.

Two links do lead elsewhere — our Airbnb listing and a map of the address — but only if you click them, and those sites then apply their own policies.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a stay are deleted after twelve months. Where you do stay with us, we keep the booking record for six years, which is the period Revenue requires for business records.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see what we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy in a portable form. Write to hello@55malinroad.ie and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. We will not apply a materially different use to information already collected without telling you first.
